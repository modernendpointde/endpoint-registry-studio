# Code of conduct

The Endpoint Registry Studio community is committed to a respectful, harassment-free project for everyone. Be constructive, assume good intent, respect differing experience and viewpoints, and focus criticism on work rather than people.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized language or attention, deliberate intimidation, publishing private information, and sustained disruption. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when conduct threatens a safe, productive community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers must avoid conflicts of interest and prioritize the safety of affected participants.
